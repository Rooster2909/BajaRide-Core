## 1. Executive Reading

Of the 8 critical blocks tracked below, **supply, competition, and pricing** now have enough external evidence to stop treating them as unknowns. **Demand** has real external evidence (cruise tourism volume, nature/adventure tourism share) but **BajaRide still has zero of its own customers, bookings, or conversion data** — that distinction must never collapse. **Economics** has real price/duration/capacity data but is missing provider costs, margins, and an accepted take rate. **Legal** has the applicable framework identified but not applied to BajaRide's specific model.

This is a reasonable framing **once genuinely reflected** in `08-Investors/Investor-Base-Document.md` — see Section 5 below for exactly which sections that touches.

## 5. Demand Evidence (EVIDENCIA EXTERNA — not BajaRide traction)

- Ensenada registered **360 cruise ship calls and approximately 1.3 million passengers in 2025**, a **41.7% increase in passengers vs. 2024**.
- Nature/adventure tourism appears as **22.2%** of stated travel motivation in the cited state tourism profile (2023), with average trip spend of **$6,595 MXN**.

**What this is**: real evidence that tourism activity and a nature/adventure-tourism segment exist at meaningful scale.
**What this is not**: evidence that any of those travelers want, know about, or would book a BajaRide experience. `Assumptions-Register.md` MARKET-M01 and MARKET-M02 remain `HYPOTHESIS` — this section gives them better supporting evidence, not a validated status.

## 7. Legal Framework Identified (EVIDENCIA EXTERNA — not a legal opinion)

Confirms the same regulatory areas already tracked in `03-Legal/Legal-Structure-Overview.md`: Ley General de Turismo + RNT, Baja California tourism framework, federal consumer protection, data protection, provider/activity/insurance/liability requirements. No new legal conclusion — this corroborates, rather than replaces, `03-Legal/Insurance-Legal-Research-Reference-2026.md`.

**Specific questions this research says a real attorney should answer** (kept because they're concrete and usable): does the ATV/UTV provider need RNT and under which category; does BajaRide itself need a specific registration as a digital booking intermediary; what insurance is mandatory for the operator vs. what the platform should require; how is liability split between platform and operator; what minimum clauses the provider contract needs.

## 9. Recommended Next Steps to Close the Real Gaps (4–8 week horizon)

1. **Customer validation**: 5–10 structured interviews with tourists who have already done ATV/UTV in Ensenada (port, hotels, or via existing tour operators). Goal: how they booked, what worked/frustrated them, real willingness-to-pay.
2. **Pilot bookings**: a 4–6 week pilot with 1–2 real providers, BajaRide as the sales channel (web/WhatsApp/landing page), provider operates the experience. Measure traffic → leads → bookings, CAC, and satisfaction.
3. **Real unit economics**: once the pilot runs, replace `Financial-Assumptions-Register.md` TBD values with observed ones (provider variable cost, net margin, accepted commission; BajaRide's CAC and effective take rate).
4. **Focused legal validation**: take the specific questions in Section 7 to an actual tourism-law attorney in Baja California — not a generic legal review.

# Risk Register

**Document Status**: ACTIVE  
**Last Updated**: September 2026  
**Classification**: RISK MANAGEMENT / GOVERNANCE

---

## I. Purpose

This document identifies, assesses, and tracks risks that could impact BajaRide's ability to launch, operate, scale, and succeed. Risks are assessed for probability and impact, and mitigation strategies are defined.

---

## II. Risk Assessment Levels

| Level | Probability | Impact | Action |
|-------|-------------|--------|--------|
| **CRITICAL** | Any | Existential | Immediate mitigation required |
| **HIGH** | High | Major | Active mitigation; regular review |
| **MEDIUM** | Medium | Moderate | Monitor; have mitigation plan |
| **LOW** | Low | Minor | Monitor; no immediate action |
